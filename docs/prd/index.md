# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - SkipperClub

## 1. Product Overview

SkipperClub is a platform connecting skippers organizing sailing cruises with people looking for spots on board.
The application enables detailed user profiles, publishing cruise listings with precise criteria, searching and filtering offers, managing applications, running real-time communication, sharing sailing experiences through posts, and exchanging mutual reviews after the cruise.
The platform combines crew recruitment with a sailing social network, featuring AI-assisted content creation, real-time messaging, and community-driven content.

## 2. User Problem

Currently, crew assembly happens chaotically in Facebook groups, which requires numerous messages, compromises, and manual verification of expectations.
Sailors lack a dedicated platform that enables:

- Precise filtering of cruises by sailing style, experience, budget, and exclusions (smoking, alcohol, children, pets).
- Transparent profiles showing participant skills, credentials, and preferences.
- A trusted rating system based on actual sailing experiences.
- Safe, centralized communication (private, group, cruise-specific) in one place.
- Easy sharing of sailing experiences, tips, and location-specific information.
- Organized regional content for marinas, routes, and sailing conditions.

This results in long recruitment times, the risk of conflicts on the yacht, fragmented sailing knowledge, and untapped potential for sailing community building.

## 4. Product Boundaries

### In Scope

- Crew recruitment and cruise organization
- Social networking for sailors (profiles, friends, posts)
- Real-time messaging and notifications
- Post-cruise blind review system
- AI-assisted content creation (cruise drafts, voice transcription)
- Community-driven content (posts, comments, reactions, validity voting)
- Location services (regions, geocoding, place search)
- Media management (photos, videos with metadata)
- Multi-language support (English, Polish)
- Email notifications and delivery tracking
- Admin-managed platform invitations

### Out of Scope

- Online payments and transactions
- Charter company integrations
- Automated crew matching algorithms
- Transport booking and organization
- Official license verification and certification
- Monetization features (premium subscriptions, paid listings)
- Multi-tenant or white-label deployments

## 6. Success Metrics

### Cruise Recruitment

1. 70% of cruises assemble a full crew within 60 days of publication.
2. The average participant rating (after the cruise) is greater than 3.0 out of 5.0.
3. 50% of active users publish or join another cruise within 12 months.

### Platform Engagement

4. 60% of users complete their profile with sailing credentials and preferences.
5. Active users send an average of 10 messages per week.
6. 40% of cruise participants publish at least one post about their sailing experience.

### Community Health

7. Less than 5% of posts are reported for violations.
8. 80% of submitted reviews are published (both parties complete their reviews).
9. The platform maintains an average of 100 new posts per week across all regions.
